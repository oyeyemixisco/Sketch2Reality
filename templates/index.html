Four models/configurations are executed sequentially. This is more intensive than a single generation.

Pixel difference shows whether an image changed, not whether it improved.

✳ Why transparency matters

The Explain tab reports actual preprocessing choices, model IDs and settings returned from Kaggle. Heuristic explanations do not reveal the model's internal reasoning.
